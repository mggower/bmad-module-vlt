(`factory/studies/ST-10-continuity-is-actor-held-by-default.md`, cause A) — cited, not restated.

## The claim

The module already distinguishes partners defined by a **mode of work across the vault** from
partners defined by a **domain practice**, and uses the distinction to decide which hand a
partner wears. It does not use it to decide whether the partner remembers. Both kinds get the
same four memory files and the same default posture. `ST-10` argues the continuity of the first
kind is mostly state of the corpus and belongs there; this filing is the contract-level cut that
would follow, if the pilot bears it out.

## Grounding (module source, `d3111e0`)

- **The cut, already drawn.** `skills/vlt-mint/SKILL.md:55`: *"Most partners are horizontal —
  **function** partners (librarian / researcher / creative) defined by a mode of work that spans
  domains. A vertical (**domain**) partner … runs a domain verb — most often the **longitudinal
  loop**."*
- **Nothing about memory hangs on it.** `:132` seeds every partner `memory: full` by default;
  the enum is `full | reflexes` (`frontmatter.md:201`); the contract's Beat 1 (`:196`) branches
  on the field, never on the archetype.
- **The posture and the path both shipped in v0.19.0.** `memory: reflexes` skips the `## Bond` /
  `## Self` reads (contract `:196`); `demote` moves material *"from partner memory to the wiki by
  Librarian hand-off"* (`:246`); cold-by-choice is per-summon (`:208`). Nothing new is needed to
  *run* a lens — only to say which roles are one.
- **The function partners' threads are instructed to hold corpus state.** Librarian sitting-end
  write (`skills/vlt-agent-librarian/SKILL.md:55`): *"what's drifting and worth watching, where
  the last lint landed, pages to watch."* Researcher (`skills/vlt-agent-researcher/SKILL.md:58`):
  *"the open questions still live, the stances you took, what the user is circling or
  resisting"* — the first clause corpus state, the rest relationship state.
- **The pilot is unrun.** `factory/cycles/16-closed-loops/roadmap.md:1878–1885`: the Researcher
  stands at `full`; the flip has not been committed in `{field-vault}`; zero of five post-flip
  dives have run.

**No set was observed.** No `{field-vault}` thread was read. `ST-8`'s 2026-09-09 weight table is
corroboration only, and it is confounded: the three function partners are also the oldest and
most used.

## What this filing does not claim

- **Not the Creative.** It is a function partner by `:55` and heavy by `ST-8`, but brainstorming
  is not corpus work and no evidence here places it. Its side of the cut is the owner's to rule,
  on the owner's own report about partnerless sittings.
- **Not the heaviest practice partner.** Its weight is `ST-8`'s casebook-and-owner-model
  diagnosis, unchanged by this cut.
- **Not a teardown.** No memory file is deleted, no partner retired; a lens keeps its persona,
  charter and reflexes.

## Why it matters

- **It is the answer to the owner's standing doubt, stated so it can be wrong.** Keep partners
  for practices; make vault-subject roles lenses. The pilot either supports that or relocates the
  cause.
- **Without the cut, `memory: reflexes` stays a per-partner accident** — a field someone has to
  remember to set — rather than a consequence of what the role is for.

## Candidate shape (ideation rules — after the pilot)

1. **Hang the birth posture on the archetype.** Function partners default to `reflexes`; domain
   partners per `2026-10-06-120200`'s graduation rule. One decision about `memory:`, made once
   across both filings.
2. **Narrow what a function partner's thread may hold** to relationship state (stances,
   resistances, how this owner likes to be challenged); corpus state goes to the slots
   `2026-10-06-120100` completes. Elimination, not precedence: the thread's population shrinks.
3. **Migration by `demote`, per partner, at the owner's pace** — a groom pass that proposes
   moving existing corpus-shaped thread entries to their new homes. Existing shipped partners
   are not flipped by `vlt-upgrade`; adoption is contract-side.
4. **The Creative ruled explicitly**, either way, with the reason recorded.

## What the pilot must show first

`ST-10`'s prediction, restated as this filing's unlock: after the Researcher's flip, what it
misses is relationship-shaped, not corpus-shaped. If instead it misses corpus facts it had no
other way to find, the slots (`120100`) come before any role moves and this filing waits on that
build's field result. If it misses nothing and the owner still prefers partnerless sittings for
knowledge work, the persona — not its memory — is the cost, and this filing is the wrong fix.
