## The claim

A partner is the heaviest structure a vault can grow: a skill, four memory files, a wake cost on
every summon, and a line in every roster read. `vlt-mint` shapes *who* the partner is with care
and never asks *whether the work it will carry has happened more than once*. A second, smaller
gap rides with it: several shipped gates already tell the user exactly what would clear them, but
no rule says a gate must, so the property is a habit of individual authors.

## Why it matters

- **`ST-8`'s control group is the argument.** The light practice partners work; the question a
  mint should ask is whether this one will be a practice or a one-off wearing a persona.
- **A default of `full` makes the expensive posture free to choose** and the cheap one a
  deliberate act — the reverse of how the cost falls.
- **A gate that does not say what clears it reads as a wall**; one that does is a next step. The
  module already believes this in three places.

## Candidate shape (ideation rules)

1. **A recurrence question in `vlt-mint` Phase 1**, for a new partner only: name the prior
   sittings (session notes, by path) where this loop was run without the partner. Below a stated
   number, mint proceeds only as `memory: reflexes`, or the owner overrides in words and the
   override is recorded in the mint decision log. A question and a recorded answer — not a block.
2. **`memory: full` is graduated to, not born with** — a new partner starts at `reflexes`; the
   move to `full` is an owner act at a natural seam. Existing partners are untouched (durability).
3. **One contract sentence under *Honest reporting***: a gate that refuses states what is
   missing and the act that would clear it. Sites that already do are conforming; no skill is
   re-ack'd for a prose rule.
4. **Sources, not points, for a new wiki page** — left to ideation whether this belongs here or
   with `2026-10-06-120100`; it touches `vlt-ingest:108` only.
