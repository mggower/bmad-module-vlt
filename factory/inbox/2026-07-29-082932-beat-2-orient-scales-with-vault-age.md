# Beat 2 orient is unbounded — an aging-vault tax that worsens silently

_Filed 2026-07-29 from the **factory**, prompted by a field signal: owner-observed token expense in
partner sessions on the work-machine consumer vault. Converged out of a token-efficiency ideation
session (`_output/brainstorming/brainstorming-session-2026-07-29-0804.md`, candidate C3); grounding
measured against module source at 9f05579. Owner elected to file (2026-07-29)._

## The claim

Beat 2 of the activation ritual reads the vault's live state with no explicit bounds: `{index}` in
full, `{log}` bounded only by the word "recent", plus `{backlog}`, the partner's `thread.md`, its
open `_agent/dispatch.md` slice, and its capabilities folder. Every one of these **grows with vault
age** — and two of them grow as a direct function of the module's own outputs (every sitting appends
to `{log}`; every ingest grows `{index}`). The boot cost of a partner session therefore rises
monotonically over a vault's life, with no pruning discipline, no cap, and no signal that it is
happening. A mature vault pays a materially heavier orient than a fresh one for the identical
activation — which is consistent with the originating field signal that *some* partners/sessions
feel expensive while others don't.

## Why it matters

This is the **variable** half of the cost anatomy (the fixed half is the contract read, filed as
`2026-07-29-082930-*`): fixing the boot whale while leaving orient unbounded means expense returns
with age, and the regression is invisible because nothing measures it (see companion filing
`2026-07-29-082933-*`). It is also the failure mode that field vaults hit *last* — fresh installs
(and factory tests) never feel it, which is exactly why it warrants a filing rather than trust.

## Field-contingency note

The claim's mechanism is ship-verifiable (the read list has no bounds — grep above), but the
**magnitude** is field-contingent: how big `{index}`/`{log}` actually get, and whether they dominate
a real session, is answerable only from a mature vault. The work vault is the natural evidence
source; the load-manifest filing proposes the instrument.

## Candidate dispositions (for capture to rule — not pre-empted here)

- **(a) Explicit bounds in the Beat 2 instruction.** Last-N `{log}` entries (N stated, not
  "recent"), `{index}` headings/summary tier rather than full read, `{backlog}` top slice. Cheap,
  three-file edit, but the right N is a guess until measured.
- **(b) Derived orient digest.** A compiled "state of the vault" snapshot (index summary + open
  items) that end-of-sitting or lint maintains; Beat 2 reads the digest. Derive-first-shaped, but a
  new artifact with a freshness obligation — heavier than (a).
- **(c) Measure first, bound second.** Ship the load manifest (companion filing), collect one round
  of field numbers from the work vault, then set bounds from evidence. Slowest, safest.
- **(d) Pruning discipline for the growing surfaces.** State when `{log}` rolls over / archives and
  when `thread.md` prunes — attacks the growth itself rather than the read. Composes with any of
  the above.

Preference, weakly held: **(c) then (a)**, with **(d)** considered alongside — bounds chosen from
one round of real numbers beat bounds chosen now, and the instrument is wanted anyway.
