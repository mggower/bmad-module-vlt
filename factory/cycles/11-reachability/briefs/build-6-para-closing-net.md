# Build #6 — the PARA closing net

A11-6's gap: both shipped `para_*` nets are individually correct and jointly blind. A PARA
file carrying *almost* the vocabulary — `type: note`, `author: creative` — is outside
`para_status_unknown`'s per-type enums (no such type, so no enum to be outside of) and
outside `para_missing_attestation`'s `author: agent|hybrid` key, so it lands in neither
world and only executor judgment surfaced the live instance. D1 ruled this the batch's one
**detection** case (the failure mode is not-knowing — no write-time rule can reach a writer
who doesn't know the vocabulary), so this build ships the closing nets:
**`para_type_unknown`** and **`para_author_unknown`**, each with an R3 stated legal
response.

The build **absorbs Q1c**: build-2 made `{resources}` (outside its `{wiki}` subtree) a full
PARA zone with area-axis enums, so the `para_*` population and the container carve-out at
`checks.md:16` extend to `{resources}` — and per roundtable A3 the **`{wiki}` Librarian-only
exclusion lands in the same `checks.md` edit, never separately** (build-2 shipped only the
contract half of the carve-out; an exclusion written before the population contained
`{resources}` would have been an edit into a sentence that didn't exist yet). Unguarded, the
Q1 grant would ship a mass false-positive into the very nets this build is building — the
exclusion is correctness scope, not tidiness.

All rejected alternatives in the parent filing and the rulings are settled — do not
re-litigate: the widen-the-vocabulary direction is closed by D1 (the closing net is the
ruled mechanism); the net-side `complete` carve-out is closed by A2 (enum-side, shipped);
the consult sibling was answered NO at capture (council-only was build-5's concern; no
consult scope exists here at all).

## Brief-time dispositions

1. **The widening branch is not taken — build-6 does not grow.** The roadmap's brief-time
   question ("if the vocabulary widens: are partner names legal `author:` values, and are
   non-artifact types legal residents of container folders?") is conditioned on a branch D1
   forecloses: the ruled mechanism is the **detection net**, not a deliberate vocabulary
   widening. For the record, the sub-answers as this brief rules them (headless-mode
   judgment, recorded inline): **partner names are not legal `author:` values** — the
   contract's authorship-honesty boundary (`vault-operating-contract.md:66`) is
   `human | agent | hybrid`, and partner *identity* already has two shipped homes
   (attestation `verified_by:` per `write-verification.md`, and per-entry `(<partner>)`
   attribution on container records per `extraction.md:181`); the net therefore **flags**
   an off-enum `author:` and its legal response rewrites toward the honest enum value — it
   never "maps" a partner name to `agent` silently (never-auto-fix is the router's standing
   rule). **Non-artifact types are not legal residents of PARA folders** as such: an
   off-vocabulary `type:` is either declared vault-grown schema in
   `{overlays}/extraction.overlay.md` (declare-at-birth, `extraction.md:115`), retyped to
   the shipped vocabulary, or the file is relocated out of PARA — the three routes the
   legal response states.
2. **Pre-adoption posture: the sibling posture.** Both new nets are **informational, never
   a violation, for files whose `created` predates convention adoption** — exactly
   `para_status_unknown`'s and `unattested_write`'s stated posture (`checks.md:16-17`).
   Loud only on post-adoption files. A new finding family with a stricter retroactive
   posture than its siblings would be a backfill sweep by another name, which the
   coexistence posture (`extraction.md:115`) forbids.
3. **Report routing: `flag_for_human`.** Both nets are judgment-bearing (is this vault-grown
   vocabulary to declare, or a mistake to fix?), so they route beside their siblings in the
   report's `flag_for_human` block — never `fix_now`, never auto-fixed.
4. **`vlt-lint-full.js` is not edited.** The workflow's report skeleton emits an empty
   `para_missing_attestation: []` slot for shape-completeness (`vlt-lint-full.js:475-477`)
   but has never emitted `para_status_unknown` — the PARA scan is the SKILL's own
   jurisdiction (`full-scale.md:10`, "stay yours; fill those report slots from your own
   pass"). The new nets follow the `para_status_unknown` precedent: SKILL-filled slots, no
   workflow edit — this also avoids touching an E5 ack surface (`// depends_on:` header)
   for no behavioral reason.
5. **R1 (interim posture): not applicable.** The mechanism ships with the rule — the nets
   *are* checks in the catalog the lint agent executes; nothing ships ahead of its
   mechanism.
6. **A13 mechanics.** Build-2's BUILT status records the `vlt-lint` `extraction@6` ack
   **PROVISIONAL** — textually bipartite-consistent, behaviorally false until these
   `checks.md` edits land. This brief owns the bipartite verification (Verification 2
   below); the builder **records the provisional RESOLVED** in this brief's BUILT status,
   beside the Group E result — the two-line closure the roundtable priced ("build-6's brief
   owns the bipartite verification, and release-gate exposure is nil since one release
   ships both").
