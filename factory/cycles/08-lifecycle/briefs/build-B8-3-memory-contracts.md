staleness contract (A8-3, GAP CONFIRMED). The field evidence: a partner corrected three
times in one sitting on knowledge *already recorded in its own files*; one standing read
loaded as four stacked versions; a manual groom cut 63% of 48.1K as misrouted or dead —

latest-form-only reads with falsifiers, git-as-archive via an `archive:` frontmatter

shape: a typed promotion ladder with named verbs, a reflex layer reachable by the whole
minted fleet, thread lifecycle rules at birth, and correction-as-signal — plus the two
arc-wide seatings the roundtable assigned here: the safety model's shipped home (A6) and
the single `frontmatter.md` bump defining both hygiene watermark fields (A7).

read-before-brief pin was honored against primary evidence: the vlt-core groom prototype
at commit `b00db0a` (`reflexes.md`, the pre/post-groom `thread.md`/`identity.md`, the
