# Build #B9-2 — the three standing rules

Arc 9 is the boundary arc, and this build lands its three governing rules **early, ahead of
the builds they govern** (roadmap, *Why B9-2 is its own slot*): **Q9**, the durable-host
doctrine (a vault-local addition lands only where the base declares a carve-out — never in a
file the module overwrites on update); **D5**, the precedence posture (where two shipped
rules overlap, eliminate the overlap by narrowing a population; state precedence only where
populations cannot be cut apart); and **D6**, enforcement-ships-with-widening (a change that
widens what may be written — or states a new rule constraining it — ships with its
enforcement in the same build, or states its interim posture as a real enforcement
declaration). Ideation ruled them one governance edit; the roundtable's **Dispute 2**
re-ruled the siting as **three sitings under one build, split by audience** (Paige,
owner-ruled): D5 to the factory's `CLAUDE.md`, Q9 to the vault operating contract, D6 to
`frontmatter.md`'s *Enforcement declaration*. B9-1 shipped first and is D5's **worked
example, not its home** (roundtable A19); per A11 this brief re-reads B9-1's shipped text
against D5 as authored — the re-read is recorded in disposition 6 and it **conforms**.

All rejected alternatives in the parent filings and rulings are settled — do not
re-litigate. In particular: no single durable-host mechanism (Q9, Round 1); the
module-owned-vs-not axis is dead (A4 — it outlawed B9-4's own fix); D6-as-widenings-only is
dead (A1); the governance-bundle home for D5 is dead (Dispute 2 — its audience is
contractually forbidden to execute it, `vault-operating-contract.md:104`); Dr. Quinn's
siting dissent is on record and answered by A5 for this rule.

The governance bundle SSoT is **only** `skills/vlt-setup/assets/governance/_meta/`
(CLAUDE.md standing rule). Every governance edit below is to that tree; no second copy is
created anywhere.
