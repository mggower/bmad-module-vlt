# Build #3 — the carrier and what rides it

## Intent

A16-3 asked what a session with no partner reads, and answered *a paragraph, not the rules*: the
rule-card is loaded by exactly one reader — the summoning ritual (`vault-operating-contract.md:194`)
— so the owner's escape from the partner loop is also an escape from the rulebook. Build 2 named the
partnerless sitting and said what it **owes**; it was deliberately mechanism-free about what it
**reads** (D8/A6), because what it reads was an open runtime question. `S-4` closed that question on
2026-09-17: `@path` imports in a vault-root `CLAUDE.md` load for **every** session kind probed —
interactive, headless `claude -p`, an in-session summon, an Agent-tool subagent, a workflow-`agent()`
subagent, and a session whose cwd is a vault subdirectory — transitively, resolved from the vault
root, while a pointer **without** `@` loaded in none of them. This build makes the vault's
`CLAUDE.md` the carrier: two import lines, the card and the vault rung, single-homed as text and
delivered per session kind.

Two tenants ride it (D6). The **`partner: none` sitting** build 2 named gains its carriage without a
line of build 2's paragraph being edited — that is the whole point of writing it owes-only. The
**delegate runner** (A16-7) is the second: a partnerless, depth-1 engine that runs a bounded
`vlt-research` dive for a caller who never leaves the wheel, and returns a typed result *in the same
sitting*. Today the only routes out of a one-fact gap are read-and-cite (no note, nothing findable
later), a consult (transfers nothing — `conventions/consult.md:27`), or a relay that ends the
sitting (`references/consult.md:63`). The 2×2 the contract already reasons on at `:300` names three
cells and leaves one empty: *work moves, wheel stays*. This build fills it.

A16-6 rides the same carrier from the other side (`ST-9`): no shipped sentence says whether
`{research}` is a shared lane, so partners inferred the nearest rule and now enforce it. Five
one-sentence dispositions state the lane, put a pointer line on the rung every partner reads, fix the
Creative's route-away cue, disambiguate the hygiene row, and extend read-and-cite to the web with its
blind spot named and — per roundtable A15 — given the one visible half it can have: a denominated
`web_cited_entries:` census line.

**All rejected alternatives in the parent filings are settled — do not re-litigate.** In particular:
the `SessionStart` hook is **not** the carriage (the harvest showed it misses in-session subagent
spawns entirely); `op` stays single-tenant (`research`) — Victor's generalize-now dissent was
rejected by the population cut and conceded at the table; `memory: none` is out of scope (build 2,
D-9); and delegate is a **sibling** of consult, never "a consult that does work" (`consult@2` is the
shape `ST-9` rejected).

converted into a narrowing.** *(Ruling: Q6 + roundtable A5 (ii).)* The `promise:` line's *every

**2. No engine-prompt pointer ships.** *(Ruling: D6 + roundtable A5.)* The carrier is the **pointer
pair** (card + rung), single-homed as text once; a *delivery* per session kind is not a second

neither import nor hook, and there is none. So `vlt-delegate.js` carries **no** card/rung text and no
pointer line at all — the runner's session receives them from the vault's `CLAUDE.md` like every

# Vault

@_meta/vault-rule-card.md

**Why.** A16-3 disposition 1; `S-4` `verdict: proceed`. This is the carrier — the pointer pair,
