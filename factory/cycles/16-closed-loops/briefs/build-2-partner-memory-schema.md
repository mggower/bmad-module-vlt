# Build #2 — the partner-memory schema

## Intent

Partner memory is the one layer of the vault whose **only reader is its writer**. A partner writes
`## Self` freely and ungated (contract `:217`), reads it back whole in Beat 1 (`:196`), and nothing
between those two acts bounds it, authors it from outside, moves anything down, or lets the owner
summon the partner without it. The ladder only lifts (`:228-238`, `:240`); the one instrument that
sees the mass reports a fleet byte total nothing is wired to (`vlt-vitals.py:242`); the schema has a
slot for an actor the contract never names (`frontmatter.md:167`). This build opens that loop from
outside, in one `frontmatter@14` → `@15` bump: a **budget** a wire trips on, a **charter slot** the
owner fills, a **demote** verb with a rung to demote onto, a **cold summon**, and a **named
partnerless sitting** (roadmap §The through-line; captures A16-3 direction 2, A16-4 whole, A16-5
whole; cause `ST-8` A/B/C).

**All rejected alternatives in the parent filings are settled — do not re-litigate.** In particular:
`memory: none` is narrowed out of the enum this cycle (dispute D-9, Victor's dissent on record); the
lint half of A16-5 (1) is dropped for a render-surface guard (dispute D-4); the mint-time `## Self`
seed is retired (dispute D-11); the wired Beat 1 set keeps SKILL.md in it (dispute D-1, Builder's
dissent on record); `cold` is its own `sitting:` field and never a `partner:` modifier (Q13); the
budget lives once, in the seed wire's `threshold:` (A1 (i)). None of these re-opens here.

**Three partners are over the bound on the day this ships** (librarian 22,144, football-analyst
19,394, creative 16,021 — the re-measured table under Disposition 2). That is the instrument doing
its job, it is stated in the acceptance, and it is **not** a defect (A1 (v), Amelia).
