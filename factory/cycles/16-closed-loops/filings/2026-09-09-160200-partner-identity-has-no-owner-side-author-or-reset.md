# Partner identity has no owner-side author, no reset, and no cold summon — the partner writes, is, and reads itself

_Filed 2026-09-09 by the **factory**, from an owner problem-solving session on the partner model
(`bmad-cis-problem-solving`; session artifact gitignored). Evidence is `{field-vault}` read-only plus
module source at `5ca31f0`. Kind: `candidate`. Cause: **`ST-8`**
(`factory/studies/ST-8-always-read-memory-has-no-budget-and-no-owner.md`, cause B and the
amplifying cause) — cited, not restated. Owner elected to file._

## The claim

`## Self` is written by the partner, about the partner, in the partner's voice, at sitting end — and
loaded as instruction on every activation. Nothing in `identity.md` is the owner's. When the drift
goes somewhere the owner did not want, the exits are `vlt-groom` (approval-gated, one partner, and
upward-biased — see `2026-09-09-160100`) or a council-gated SKILL.md rebirth via `vlt-mint`. There is
no act that says *start this partner over under my terms* and no posture that says *summon this
partner without its history*. The owner's report: the field vault's football-analyst "has become
counter-productive … gone through a few grooming cycles already, but ultimately the identity is not
aligned with what I am looking for," and the Researcher's statefulness is suspected of narrowing
research breadth.

## Grounding (module source, `5ca31f0`)

- **Self is partner-authored and ungated.** Contract `:217`: "`## Self` — lightweight, **ungated**
  identity drift … A partner writes this freely, like a `## Bond` note." No owner-writable section
  exists in the `identity.md` schema (`frontmatter.md:185–193`: `partner`, `name`, dates, `groomed`,
  `archive`).
- **The two-tier line has nothing in the middle.** Contract `:246`: "Drift in `## Self` is free and
  reversible. Changing *who the partner fundamentally is* … a deliberate, council-gated edit to its
  SKILL.md (via `vlt-mint`)." *Reversible* names no verb: groom's three (`:240`) are promote /
  compress / retire, each item-wise; `skills/vlt-groom/SKILL.md` has no whole-section reset.
- **Three activation postures, none cold-by-choice.** Contract *Activation ritual* `:192–208` names
  cold start (first meeting), cold × headless (defer the ceremony), and partner-invoked (skip the
  greeting). Every one of them reads `identity.md` if it has content. There is no sanctioned way to
  summon a mature partner without its Self, Bond, and Thread.
- **Statefulness is uniform.** Nothing in `frontmatter.md`'s partner-memory schema or the contract
  lets a partner (or a mint) declare *how* stateful it is; every partner runs the full three-file
  loop. `vlt-track`-shaped partners need it; the breadth role does not.
- **The field evidence of the loop.** The Researcher's `## Self` opens: "**The correction-of-framing
  edge — my signature move, six proven target classes**" followed by "Target classes 7–13 for the
  correction edge." A self-theory, loaded as instruction, that names contrarian reframing as the
  partner's identity. The football-analyst's `## Self` is 28 dated first-person failure narratives
  (fragment quoted in `2026-09-09-160100`). The owner's most productive sittings for new thinking are
  the ones with **no** partner summoned, "because it removes the biases from the partners themselves."

## Why it matters

- **A reinforcing loop with no governor.** Act → record a self-lesson → load it → act accordingly.
  Gain rises with use, so the partners the owner leans on hardest drift fastest, and the only
  correction is the owner's attention at groom time, one partner per act.
- **The owner has no line in the file.** The one artifact that says who a partner is *with this
  user* has no place for what the user wants it *for*.
- **The escape is ungoverned** (`2026-09-09-160000`): skipping the partner is the only fresh-eyes move,
  and it sheds the rulebook.

## Candidate shape (ideation rules)

1. **An owner-side author: `## Charter`.** A short, owner-written (or minted, then owner-editable)
   section at the top of `identity.md` — *what I want this partner for*. Read first in Beat 1. Owner
   write posture (the same `writers:` test PARA containers use); `vlt-groom` checks `## Self` against
   it and proposes demotion of off-charter material; `vlt-lint` flags partner edits to it.
2. **A cheap reset: `vlt-groom --reset self|bond|thread`.** Retires a whole section by reference —
   `archive:` watermark, git history — leaving `name` and the charter. Approval-gated, council-free
   (a lossless-by-reference act, which the contract's *Hygiene* section already classes as
   council-free, `:304`). Durability holds: nothing is destroyed.
3. **A cold summon.** A fourth activation posture: "summon the Researcher, cold" → Beat 1 reads
   SKILL.md + charter + rule-card + rung only; no Self/Bond/Thread; the sitting is tagged `cold`,
   writes its note and log line, and writes **no** identity or thread drift. Per-summon, never
   per-partner, so continuity is a visible choice.
4. **Statefulness as a posture** (pilot, not fleet): `identity.md` frontmatter gains
   `memory: full | reflexes | none`; `vlt-setup` seeds `full` for every existing partner (opt-in,
   durable); `vlt-mint` asks at mint. Pilot `reflexes` on the Researcher with a breadth measure —
   distinct source domains per dive over the five dives before and after.

## Acceptance (draft)

- **Ship-verifiable:** `## Charter` in the `identity.md` schema with its write posture; reset

- **Field-contingent:** the football-analyst reset under a fresh charter and reported aligned by the
