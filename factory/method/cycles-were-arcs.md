# Cycles 1–10 were called arcs

Ruled 2026-08-23 (rulings R2 + R4, going-public design record; owner-ratified): the
lifecycle's unit of work is a **cycle** — one turn of the evolution loop. Cycles 1–10
*were* called arcs and stay so written everywhere they already appear: the closed
roadmaps under `factory/cycles/`, the dated provenance citations in shipped code, the
identifiers (`A4-…`, `B10-…` — the letters mean *amendment* and *build*, never arc).
Rewriting the record would falsify when decisions were made. The rename is
forward-only: Cycle 11 onward, the noun is cycle, branches are `cycleN-vX.Y.Z`, and
milestone-style titles read `Cycle 11 — <Title>`.
